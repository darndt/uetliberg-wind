About the model

Flow: a mass-consistent diagnostic wind model (same family as WindNinja). A log-profile wind is forced to flow over and around the real terrain on a 160 × 160 × 44 grid (≈ 88 m cells, 25 m layers); it is solved in your browser. Direction and speed changes are instant because the solution is linear in the inflow.

Lee: such models cannot produce flow separation, so lee zones are added geometrically: a point is sheltered when upwind terrain rises above it steeper than about 1:16 (full wake at 1:4, reaching ≈ 2.3 km downwind). There the mean wind is reduced and tracers get turbulent.

Soaring band: where air rises faster than a glider sinks (1.1 m/s) somewhere between 25 and 190 m above ground, outside the lee. Orange: lift is there but the wind exceeds 33 km/h.

Not included: thermals, valley winds, rotors, gust fronts, time evolution. It is a visual aid for understanding terrain flow — not a flight-planning tool.
